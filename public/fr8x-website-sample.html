COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

FR8X operates on an open-liquidity model. We do not take cuts or commissions from forwarder freight margins. Shippers pay a nominal listing fee (₹180 for Premium, ₹300 for Standard) to initiate high-intent reverse auctions, ensuring only genuine container demand enters the marketplace.
During an active reverse auction, forwarders see their relative position (e.g. Rank #1 Leader, Rank #2 Contender) without seeing the identity or exact bid amounts of competitors. This prevents predatory undercutting while ensuring the shipper receives true market price discovery.
In India, every entity is validated against active GSTN registrations, DGFT IEC codes, PAN records, and DGS Multimodal Transport Operator (MTO) certificates. Globally, we verify company registration numbers, IATA cargo codes, and authorized corporate domains.
