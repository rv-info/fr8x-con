## 2. Store Descriptions
### Short Description (80 characters)
Enterprise B2B freight reverse auctions, ocean/air rate intelligence & messaging.

### Full Description
FR8X-CON is the premier enterprise logistics reverse auction and rate benchmark platform connecting Freight Forwarders, Shipping Lines (MLOs), NVOCCs, Exporters, Importers, and Customs Brokers worldwide.

**Key Features**:
- **Reverse Auctions**: Create freight tenders and receive competitive transparent bids in real-time.
- **Ocean & Air Rates Intelligence**: Benchmark rates across 500+ global sea ports and ICD terminals with live currency conversion.
- **Enterprise Messaging & Contact Directory**: Connect with approved B2B logistics partners with end-to-end messaging and document sharing.
- **Feed & Industry Collaboration**: View verified trade updates, container availability, and logistics job postings.
- **Excel & Document Viewer**: Preview spreadsheet rate cards inline with DOM-optimized 8-row previews and full file viewing.
