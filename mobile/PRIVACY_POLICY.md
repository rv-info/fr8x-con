# Privacy Policy — FR8X-CON Enterprise Logistics Mobile Application

**Effective Date**: July 29, 2026  
**Publisher**: FR8X Logistics Technologies Pvt Ltd (`support@fr8x.in`)

---

## 1. Data Collected
FR8X-CON collects enterprise business data required to facilitate freight reverse auctions, rate benchmarking, cargo booking requests, and communication between freight forwarders, shipping lines, exporters, and importers:
* **Account Credentials**: Business email, user name, company name, corporate tax identification (GSTN/CIN/IEC).
* **Logistics Operational Data**: Origin/Destination ports, container specifications, freight rates, and reverse auction bids.
* **Device & Security Data**: Encrypted session tokens, IP address, device model, and OS version for App Check and single-session enforcement.

---

## 2. Use of Data
All collected information is used strictly to:
* Operate B2B reverse auctions, ocean/air rate intelligence, and cargo booking dispatch.
* Enforce account verification and security compliance (detecting unauthorized multi-device access).
* Send real-time chat notifications and auction outcome alerts.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security
* All network communications use TLS 1.3 encryption.
* Sensitive authentication tokens are stored in hardware-encrypted storage (`SecureStore`).
* We do not sell user or company data to third parties.

---

## 4. Account Deletion & Data Rights
Users may request account deletion or data export at any time by contacting `privacy@fr8x.in` or via the Profile settings within the FR8X-CON application.
