# Admin Guide — GodMode Management Console

This guide outlines the administrative actions for verifying achievements, auditing company registrations, and controlling user profiles in GodMode.

---

## 1. Entering GodMode
1. Log in with the administrator credentials:
   * **Email**: `support@fr8x.in`
   * **Password**: `QWERTY@123x`
2. You will be authenticated as a GodMode Administrator.
3. Click the **Admin** link in the left sidebar or profile menu to enter the Control Panel.

---

## 2. Managing Company Registrations
1. Navigate to the **Companies** panel inside the Admin console.
2. Select any registered logistics company.
3. Review their submitted tax documentation, including GSTN, CIN, and IEC licenses.
4. Toggle the **Verification Status** to "Verified". The company will immediately receive the green verified checkmark badge across public pages and search results.
5. If a company violates platform rules, click **Suspend** to restrict their active auctions and bidding rights.

---

## 3. Award Verifications
1. Go to the public **Awards** page.
2. For any nominated honor card, you will see a badge for **Admin Verify**.
3. Click **Admin Verify** to sign off on the achievement. Since you are in GodMode, this will instantly update the Firestore verifications record and activate the golden checkmark badge.
4. To remove an incorrect verify stamp, click the button again to toggle it.

---

## 4. Moderation & Audit Logs
* **Moderation**: Approve or remove posts flagged by community members.
* **Audit Log**: Review a timestamped history of actions taken on the system.
