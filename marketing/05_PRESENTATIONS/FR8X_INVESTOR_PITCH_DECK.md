### SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM ($200B MARKET RUNNING ON WHATSAPP & SPREADSHEETS)
- **The Fragmentation**: 60% of international ocean freight is handled by independent and mid-market forwarders who lack multinational tech systems.
- **The Friction**:
  1. *4 to 8 Hour Quoting Delay*: Pricing desks spend half their day emailing carriers for non-binding spot rates.
  2. *High Tender Loss*: Volume container tenders (20+ boxes) are lost due to lack of competitive carrier bidding and excessive demurrage risk.
  3. *Unverified Foreign Counterparties*: Cargo abandonment and unpaid detention bills cost forwarders millions annually.
  4. *Expensive Recruitment*: Forwarders pay ₹40,000+ per hire to headhunters for basic documentation clerks.
