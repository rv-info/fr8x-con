# SECTION 1: CORE FREIGHT FORWARDING & MARITIME ACRONYMS

1. **POL (Port of Loading)**: The designated geographical seaport or terminal where cargo is loaded aboard the maritime carrying vessel.
2. **POD (Port of Discharge)**: The designated seaport where ocean containers are unloaded from the vessel.
3. **FPOD (Final Place of Delivery)**: The ultimate inland terminal, dry port (ICD), or container depot where cargo is released to the consignee.
4. **FCL (Full Container Load)**: An ocean shipment where the shipper charters an entire container (`20GP`, `40GP`, `40HC`, `Reefer`) for their exclusive cargo.
5. **LCL (Less than Container Load)**: Ocean cargo that is consolidated with other shippers' goods into a single container at a Container Freight Station (CFS).
6. **NVOCC (Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier)**: An ocean freight carrier that issues its own House Bills of Lading and contracts container space from vessel-operating common carriers without owning the ships.
7. **BL / BoL (Bill of Lading)**: The legally binding maritime contract of carriage, receipt of goods, and document of title in international shipping.
8. **MBL (Master Bill of Lading)**: The ocean bill of lading issued by the mainline shipping line to the freight forwarder or NVOCC.
9. **HBL (House Bill of Lading)**: The bill of lading issued by the freight forwarder or NVOCC directly to the actual exporter or importer.
10. **VGM (Verified Gross Mass)**: Mandatory SOLAS convention regulation requiring the certified gross weight of a packed container before vessel loading.
11. **THC (Terminal Handling Charges)**: Port handling tariff covering the unmooring, crane movement, and yard positioning of a container.
    - **OTHC**: Origin Terminal Handling Charges (paid in exporter's local currency, e.g., INR).
    - **DTHC**: Destination Terminal Handling Charges (paid in importer's local currency, e.g., EUR, AED, USD).
12. **Demurrage**: Penalties levied by the shipping line while a container remains inside the marine terminal beyond the granted free time.
13. **Detention**: Penalties levied by the shipping line while a container is held by the importer outside the port terminal beyond the allowed free days.
14. **Combined Free Time**: Total contractual free days granted to an importer to clear the container through customs, destuff it, and return the empty equipment without incurring demurrage or detention.
15. **BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor)**: Floating maritime surcharge reflecting bunker fuel price fluctuations.
16. **CAF (Currency Adjustment Factor)**: Surcharge compensating carriers for currency exchange rate volatility.
17. **GRI (General Rate Increase)**: Periodic ocean freight price hikes announced across major trade lanes by carrier alliances.
18. **PSS (Peak Season Surcharge)**: Temporary seasonal fee applied during high-volume manufacturing export windows.
19. **TEU (Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit)**: Standard unit of measurement in ocean shipping equivalent to one 20-foot shipping container.
20. **FEU (Forty-foot Equivalent Unit)**: Equivalent to one 40-foot shipping container (`40GP` or `40HC`).

# SECTION 2: INCOTERMS® 2020 COMMERCIAL DEFINITIONS

- **EXW (Ex Works)**: The buyer assumes maximum risk, arranging transport, insurance, and export customs clearance from the seller's factory floor.
- **FOB (Free On Board)**: The seller clears export customs and delivers goods aboard the vessel at POL; the buyer assumes all ocean freight, destination handling, and import risk.
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)**: The seller arranges and pays ocean freight and marine cargo insurance to the destination POD; buyer handles import duties and destination clearance.
- **CFR (Cost and Freight)**: The seller pays ocean freight to POD, but the buyer is responsible for marine transit insurance.
- **DAP (Delivered at Place)**: The seller delivers cargo to the buyer's specified inland warehouse, excluding import customs duty and local taxes.
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)**: The seller assumes maximum liability, paying all freight, marine insurance, import customs duties, and doorstep delivery to the consignee.

# SECTION 3: STATUTORY ACCREDITATION & REGULATORY TERMINOLOGY

- **MTO (Multimodal Transport Operator)**: A freight logistics company legally registered under the Multimodal Transportation of Goods Act to issue through multimodal transport documents covering sea, rail, and road.
- **IATA (International Air Transport Association)**: Global airline trade association certifying accredited cargo agents authorized to issue Air Waybills (AWB).
- **FMC (Federal Maritime Commission)**: United States regulatory body governing ocean freight forwarders and NVOCCs operating in US maritime foreign commerce (OTI license).
- **CHA (Customs House Agent)**: A licensed professional entity in India authorized to clear import and export cargo through Indian Customs under the Customs Act.
- **GSTN (Goods and Services Tax Network)**: Statutory 15-digit Indian tax identification number mandatory for domestic corporate billing and input tax credit (ITC).
