# User Guide — FR8X-CON Logistics Network

This guide explains how to use the completed professional network, search, and identity features in the FR8X-CON platform.

---

## 1. Professional Profile & Picture Cropping

1. Navigate to **Profile** (or click **Edit Profile** at the top).
2. Under **Personal & Professional Info**, click **Upload Photo**.
3. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WEBP image.
4. Use the sliders to Zoom and Pan to position your photo in the circular crop mask.
5. Click **Crop & Upload**.
6. Set your designating details and custom tags, then click **Save Profile Changes**.
7. Your unique handle (e.g. `@RAJAT001`) will display under your name. Other users can view your page directly via `/profile/@RAJAT001`.

---

## 2. Managing Company Details & Tax IDs

1. Click **Company Page** in the sidebar.
2. If you don't have a company registered yet, click **Create Company Profile**.
3. Enter your company name, country, industry, and select a logo. Use the Pan/Zoom crop tool.
4. Enter your corporate credentials: GSTN (15-digit Tax ID), PAN (optional), CIN (optional), IEC code, and year established.
5. Click **Save Company Identity**. Your company will receive a handle (e.g. `@COMP-0001`). Other logistics members can view it via `/company/@COMP-0001`.

---

## 3. Connecting with Network Partners

1. Search for a professional using the Search Bar or in the Feed.
2. Click **Connect as Partner** on their card or profile.
3. Once they approve, they will appear in your network roster. You will see proximity match indicators like:
   * **Industry Match %**: Overlap of logistics tags.
   * **Mutual Partners**: Shared connections count.
4. Toggle **Follow** to prioritize their rate inquiries in your Feed.

---

## 4. Universal Search

1. Type in the top search bar to see instant autocomplete suggestions.
2. Press Enter to open the **Search Center**.
3. Select tabs to filter by **Professionals**, **Companies**, **RFQs**, or **Tags**.
4. Use the sidebar to drill down results by country, city, or verification status.
